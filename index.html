How to play

Swap number tiles until every number is in its intended position.

  • Each gold tile shows the sum required for both its row and its column.
  • Green: correct spot.
  • Yellow: correct row or correct column, but not both.
  • Gray: neither the right row nor column.
  • Drag one number onto another to swap. Gold clue tiles cannot move.

The small numbers along the right and bottom show your current sums compared with the targets.

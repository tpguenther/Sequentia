How to Play

Find the hidden seven-number sequence in six guesses.

  1. Two numbers are already revealed. These stay fixed in every guess.
  2. The first empty tile is highlighted. Tap keypad digits to type directly into it. Press Enter ↵ to move to the next tile, or use the arrows to navigate.
  3. Use ⌫ to erase a digit. Tap a filled tile to select it; its number stays until you type a replacement.
  4. When all five missing numbers are filled, tap Submit Guess to see feedback.
